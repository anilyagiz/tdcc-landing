SILICONAnnouncing TDCC Exact Core · open source

An AI chip whose every answer anyone can re-check.

Exact Core is TDCC's own accelerator, built on the open-source openTPU design. The card produces the same bits as its simulator, so any laptop can replay a job and find the first instruction a dishonest run got wrong.

Exact Core, explodedIllustration

Matrix unit

The heavy LLM math in 8-bit numbers, in a fixed order.

Vector unit

Softmax and norms with fixed rounding, so the bits never drift.

Trace log

Records every step, so a dispute points to the one step that differs.

Sequencer + DMA

One instruction per cycle; every data movement is an instruction.

How TDCC Exact works

  1. RentA renter opens a job on Solana; the USDC waits in escrow.
  2. RunA provider runs it on Exact Core and posts the answer's fingerprint.
  3. CheckAny verifier re-runs it on a laptop. A mismatch opens a challenge.
  4. SettleHonest providers get paid: 80% to the host, 20% to TDCC. A cheater loses its stake; the renter is refunded and the verifier takes a share.

Why this exists

AI runs on compute that is scattered across thousands of operators.

Demand for AI compute compounds every year, while supply sits fragmented across thousands of operators that no single team can reach. Data centers have capacity they cannot fill; AI teams have workloads they cannot place. TDCC is the grid between them.

How it works

What happens after you submit a job.

GPUs are not interchangeable. TDCC matches every job with hardware that fits its model, memory, speed, price, and reliability needs, then checks that the work was actually done.

Send a training, fine-tuning, or inference job through the API, CLI, SDK, or console.

Engineering note · September 2026

Two compact laptops. One 27B model. 80 tokens per second.

We paired two small machines for a Qwen 3.8 27B workload and moved a ~50 tok/s run to 80 tok/s, a 60% gain, without touching the weights or the intended output. The work was in the path around generation: coordinating handoffs, cutting idle time, keeping one stream moving.

Most of what we do is this kind of work: getting more out of hardware that already exists.

FAQ

Questions we get often.

What is TDCC?

An AI inference and compute network that connects AI teams with data centers and GPUs for training, fine-tuning, and inference through one API, with every result checked.

Do I need to manage a GPU?

No. Send your job through the API. TDCC handles matching, execution, failover, and verification.

Do I need a wallet or crypto?

No. You use TDCC with an API key like any AI provider. Payouts are off-chain at launch; optional on-chain settlement records via Avalanche and Solana are planned for teams that want public, auditable records.

Can data centers and GPU owners join?

Yes. Data centers and GPU operators can provide capacity and earn from accepted AI jobs.

What makes TDCC different?

We sell verified AI work, not raw GPU hours. Failed work is never billed and never paid out.

How does TDCC fit Avalanche and Solana?

TDCC stays chain-agnostic at the compute layer. Avalanche fits verifiable coordination and customizable infrastructure, while Solana fits high-throughput developer, agent, and micro-workflow experiences. They are complementary integration paths, not competing compute backends.
